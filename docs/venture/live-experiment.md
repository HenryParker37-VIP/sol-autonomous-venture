# Seven-day live experiment

## Entry gate

Before enabling the live experiment, verify the public landing page, accurate $5 pricing, PayPal link, manual payment-confirmation path, delivery template, cost ceiling, emergency stop, and outreach/posting targets.

## Daily loop

1. Review activity and replies.
2. Prepare no more than three platform posts, twenty total personalized DMs, and thirty public comments across the sprint.
3. Manually review every message and submission.
4. Record inquiries and orders without exposing unnecessary personal data.
5. Confirm payment manually before delivery.
6. Deliver within 24 hours and record one revision if used.
7. Review evidence before changing the offer.

## Stop and pivot rules

After 30 qualified impressions or 10 relevant conversations with no inquiry, inspect traffic, positioning, price, trust, and product value. Make one focused change and record it. Stop a channel immediately if it triggers a warning, rejects promotion, or receives negative feedback.
