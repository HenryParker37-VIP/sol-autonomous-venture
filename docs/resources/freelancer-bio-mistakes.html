Three mistakes that make a freelancer profile confusing

1. Leading with a job title

“Designer” or “writer” leaves the visitor to guess the problem you solve. Lead with the kind of client or situation you understand.

2. Listing skills without a result

Tools matter after context. Explain what becomes easier, clearer, or more useful for the client.

3. Hiding the next step

Give one low-friction action: see a sample, send a brief, book a call, or ask a question. Do not ask for everything at once.

Free rewrite exercise: “I help [specific client] [make a useful change] without [common frustration].” Keep it honest and remove promises you cannot prove.
