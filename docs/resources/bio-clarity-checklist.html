Five reasons a profile bio feels unclear

Use this checklist before asking anyone to rewrite your profile. It helps you spot the problem yourself first.

1. It names a role, not a buyer

“Designer” or “coach” is a starting point. Add who you help or the situation they recognize.

2. It uses abstract benefits

Words like “growth,” “clarity,” and “success” need a concrete context to mean anything.

3. It hides the next action

Tell a visitor whether to read, book, download, follow, or message you.

4. It lists tools instead of outcomes

Tools can support credibility, but the first sentence should explain the useful result.

5. It tries to speak to everyone

A narrower audience is easier for the right person to recognize.

Quick test: after reading your bio once, can a stranger answer who you help, what changes, and what should they do next?
