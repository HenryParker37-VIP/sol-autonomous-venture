Explain who you help in one sentence

A clear profile does not need to explain your whole career. It needs to help the right visitor recognize themselves quickly.

I help [specific audience] [achieve a concrete change] through [your service or approach].

Example: “I help early-stage teams turn complex products into simple, confident web experiences.” This is a sample demonstration, not a customer result.

  1. Choose one audience you can recognize.
  2. Name one practical change, not a guaranteed outcome.
  3. Remove extra services until the first sentence is easy to repeat.
