# SAMPLE DEMONSTRATIONS

All examples below are fictional demonstrations. They are not customer work, testimonials, or performance claims.

## Freelancer

Original: “Designer helping brands with creative solutions.”

Problems identified: no audience, no situation, no outcome, no reason to act.

Improved: “I help early-stage teams turn complex products into simple, confident web experiences.”

Why clearer: it names the buyer, the service context, and the result without promising revenue.

## Coach

Original: “Coach | mindset | helping you become your best self.”

Problems identified: broad audience, abstract outcome, no concrete situation.

Improved: “I help first-time managers lead calmer 1:1s and give clearer feedback.”

Why clearer: it makes the audience and practical use case obvious.

## Indie builder

Original: “Building in public. SaaS founder. DM me.”

Problems identified: no product category, no audience, no problem, vague CTA.

Improved: “Building a simple client follow-up tool for solo service businesses that lose leads in their inbox.”

Why clearer: a visitor can identify the product, target user, and problem in one read.
