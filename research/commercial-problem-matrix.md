# Commercial problem research

Research date: 2026-07-12. These are buyer-problem hypotheses grounded in observable public discussions and channels, not fabricated demand or sales evidence. The selected offer is retained only as a seven-day test because it is fast to understand, demonstrate, and fulfill at a low price.

| Buyer problem | Buyer type | Where they look | Urgency signal | Paid alternatives | Objection | Trust required | Best format | Discovery channel | Expected willingness | Why it may fail |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Profile says a role but not an outcome | Freelancer | LinkedIn, portfolio feedback, freelance forums | Visitors cannot tell what to hire them for | Copywriter, peer critique | “I can rewrite this myself” | Before/after sample and clear scope | Before/after rewrite | Helpful public critique thread where allowed | $5-$30 | Audience may be too small or already satisfied |
| Creator bio is broad and has no next step | TikTok, Reels, Shorts creator | Creator communities, platform profile | New visitors do not know why to follow | Creator coach, templates | Fear of sounding salesy | Niche-aware examples, no growth guarantee | Bio checklist + sample | Search-indexed creator clarity article | $5-$20 | Creator may value content ideas more than bio copy |
| Student profile does not show target role | Student or early-career worker | LinkedIn, campus career groups | Recruiting window or application deadline | Career coach, resume service | Privacy and personal-data concerns | Minimal data request and privacy terms | Profile headline example | Public career advice content | $5-$25 | Need may be seasonal and budget-sensitive |
| Coach lists credentials without a clear buyer | New coach | Coaching communities, public profile | Profile visits do not become calls | Positioning consultant | Concern about making unsupported claims | No outcome claims, focused scope | Offer-clarity example | Educational post about vague coaching bios | $5-$40 | Regulated or sensitive claims need exclusion |
| Consultant profile does not state who they help | New consultant | X, LinkedIn, consultant directories | Launching a new service | Brand strategist, copywriter | Wants proof of business fluency | Clear audience/outcome examples | Three bio options | Search article and permitted community reply | $5-$40 | May expect a full positioning project |
| Indie hacker bio says “building in public” only | Indie hacker | X, Indie Hackers, maker communities | Launch or waitlist needs clarity | Founder copywriter, peer feedback | Community rules against promotion | Useful critique first and transparent sample | Bio + pinned hook | SEO guide for maker profiles | $5-$25 | Community norms vary and self-promotion may be banned |
| Small business page buries service and location | Local service owner | Google Business, Instagram, local directories | Wants calls or bookings | Local marketer, agency | Needs proof it fits their trade | Localized example and no ranking promise | Service bio rewrite | Local-business clarity checklist | $5-$30 | Contact information may be private or outdated |
| Portfolio project descriptions list tools, not outcomes | Designer/developer | Portfolio feedback, web-design communities | Job search or client outreach | Portfolio reviewer, career coach | Wants detailed portfolio strategy | Public URL only and concrete edits | Project-summary before/after | Helpful public critique where allowed | $5-$35 | Buyer may need visual design help instead |
| Newsletter landing page has no clear promise | Newsletter owner | Newsletter communities, marketing forums | Subscriber growth or launch | Copywriter, newsletter consultant | Needs more than a bio | One sample and explicit scope boundary | Headline + welcome hook | “Five reasons readers bounce” article | $5-$35 | Requires access to broader funnel context |
| Freelancer intake page does not explain next action | Solo service provider | Freelance forums, portfolio sites | Leads ask repetitive questions | Process consultant, template seller | May prefer a free template | Clear deliverables and fast turnaround | CTA + intake rewrite | Free intake checklist | $5-$30 | Low willingness to pay for small copy change |

## Selection decision

`bio-profile-fix` remains the highest-probability seven-day test because it has a visible public symptom, a result that fits in one sentence, three easy-to-show sample transformations, a $5 price that lowers first-purchase risk, and a fulfilment path that does not require account access. This is a test hypothesis, not proof of demand. The experiment must pivot if the thresholds in `ops/launch-board.md` are missed.
